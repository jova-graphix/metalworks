Our Story

Built on Skill,
Driven by Quality

Akpaloo Metal Works is a Ghanaian metal fabrication business founded on a deep commitment to quality craftsmanship, precision engineering, and lasting customer relationships.

We specialise in a wide range of metalwork services — from intricate custom designs to heavy-duty structural fabrication. Every project we take on, regardless of size, receives our full skill, attention, and dedication.

Our team of experienced welders and fabricators work with a variety of metals and techniques, ensuring that every finished product meets the highest standards of durability, safety, and visual appeal.

What We Offer

Our Services

From precision welding to large-scale steel structures — comprehensive metalwork solutions for residential, commercial and industrial clients.

Welding & Metal Fabrication

Professional MIG, TIG, and arc welding services for all types of metalwork. We fabricate structural components, enclosures, frames, brackets and complex assemblies with precision and care.

  • MIG, TIG & Arc Welding
  • Structural Fabrication
  • Custom Metal Components

Gates & Doors

Custom-designed and fabricated metal gates and doors for residential and commercial properties. From elegant sliding gates to heavy-duty security doors — made to enhance your property's security and appearance.

  • Sliding & Swing Gates
  • Pedestrian Gates
  • Security Metal Doors

Security Grilles

Heavy-duty steel window and door grilles that provide robust security while maintaining an attractive appearance. Designed to deter intruders without compromising ventilation or natural light.

  • Window Security Grilles
  • Door Grilles & Burglar Bars
  • Custom Decorative Grilles

Railings & Balustrades

Beautifully crafted and structurally sound railings for staircases, balconies, terraces and walkways. We combine safety engineering with elegant design to complement any architectural style.

  • Staircase Railings
  • Balcony Balustrades
  • Handrails & Guardrails

Roofing & Steel Structures

Design and fabrication of steel roof trusses, purlins, canopies, carports and structural steel frameworks. Built to withstand Ghana's climate and engineered for long-term structural integrity.

  • Steel Roof Trusses
  • Canopies & Carports
  • Structural Steel Frames

Custom Metal Works

Have a unique idea or specific design in mind? We bring your concepts to life through bespoke metalwork — decorative pieces, signage frames, furniture frames, display stands and more.

  • Bespoke Metal Designs
  • Decorative Metalwork
  • Furniture & Display Frames

Steel Frames

Precision-fabricated steel window frames, door frames, louvre frames and structural support frames. Built for exact fit, strength and long-lasting performance in all conditions.

  • Window & Door Frames
  • Louvre Frames
  • Structural Support Frames

Repairs & Maintenance

Restore and maintain your existing metalwork to peak condition. We repair gates, grilles, railings, structural steel and all types of metal installations quickly, reliably and professionally.

  • Gate & Grille Repairs
  • Structural Repair & Reinforcement
  • Welding Repairs
